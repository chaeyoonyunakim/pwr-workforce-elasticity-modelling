PWR workforce elasticity modelling — headline results

Generated at 2026-05-12 18:17 UTC. Analytical window: financial years 2021/22 to 2025/26. Audited Trust Accounts Consolidation panel covering 263 provider-year observations across four FYs and 66–68 NHS trusts each year.

Headline estimate. Each one-unit step in cumulative agency-rule policy intensity is associated with a coefficient of -0.2867 on log Other-staff pay (≈ -28.7%; 95 % CI [-0.4338, -0.1396]; SE 0.0746; n = 262; ICS clusters n = 32). Reported coefficient name: policy_intensity_t.
Editorial caveat. The pre-trend diagnostic rejects parallel trends and the placebo p-value is 0.292 on the four-FY permutation set, so the headline must be read as a policy-period descriptive elasticity rather than a clean causal effect. The sign is robust across robustness variants; the magnitude is uncertain. See Diagnostics for detail.
